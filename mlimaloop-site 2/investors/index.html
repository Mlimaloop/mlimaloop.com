01 · The collector

It starts with one bottle, and one person.

A collector picks a bottle off a roadside in Arusha, scans it into the app, drops it at a mobile buying station, and is paid on the spot. Every time. No middleman, no waiting, no discretion.

$0.165 / kg, paid instantly through the app to mobile money.

A dedicated collector earns around $210 a month, roughly three times Tanzania's minimum wage.

Every kilo GPS-stamped and named: the chain of custody that unlocks premium ocean-bound plastic credits.

Inside the plant

From bottle to food-grade pellet.

Because feedstock stockpiles during build-out, the plant runs two shifts from day one, around seven to eight tonnes processed daily. Six stages, fully traceable, through to a food-grade reactor and a pilot yarn line.

01

Collect & sort

Named collectors, app-logged, sorted by resin and colour.

02

Shred to flake

Granulated and friction-washed; caps float off for PP recovery.

03

Hot wash

Caustic hot-wash to food-contact cleanliness; water recycled.

04

Extrude to pellet

Twin-screw extrusion into uniform, sellable rPET pellet.

05

SSP reactor

Solid-state upgrade to food-grade IV.

Value gate
06

Pilot yarn

Sample yarn for brand qualification and the textile route.

Non-food pellet sells immediately. Food-grade follows once certified, commanding premium, long-term offtake from beverage and FMCG brands.

The loop closes

Nothing is wasted.

The bottle becomes food-grade pellet and recycled fibre. What cannot be recycled becomes something else entirely, and the value keeps circulating in the community that collected it.
