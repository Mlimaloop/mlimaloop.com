The problem

A continent drowning
in its own plastic.

East Africa recycles less than three per cent of its plastic. The rest chokes waterways, cities and soil. Arusha alone throws out eight to twelve tonnes of collectable PET every day, and the city's Sakina landfill holds a stockpile of more than fifty thousand tonnes. The bottles are already here. So is the global demand for certified recycled material, driven hard by EU recycled-content law. What is missing is the infrastructure, and a fair, formal chain, to connect the two. That gap is the opportunity, and MlimaLoop is built to close it.

🌊

Ocean & waterway pollution

Arusha drains via the Pangani river to the Indian Ocean, so material collected here qualifies as high-value ocean-bound plastic.

🏭

No local processing

Despite huge collection potential, the region has almost no industrial capacity to process PET at scale. Bottles are landfilled or shipped out raw.

📦

Brand demand is surging

Mandatory recycled-content targets mean global packaging and fashion brands urgently need verified, traceable recycled supply.

🤝

No formal waste economy

Thousands of informal collectors work with no structure, no fair pricing and no protection. MlimaLoop formalises and rewards the chain.

The loop

Follow one bottle.

From a street in Arusha to a global brand, and back again. Because we stockpile feedstock during build-out, the plant runs two shifts from day one, and every kilo is logged, named and traceable through the MlimaLoop app.

The green fleet brings mobile buying stations to the community.
01
📲

The collector

A bottle is picked up, scanned into the app and paid for instantly, fairly, on the spot. A dedicated collector earns around three times the local minimum wage.

02
🛺

The fleet

Green mobile buying stations bring the depot to the community, so collectors never travel far and can sell a whole basket of materials.

03
💧

The plant

Sorted, shredded and hot-washed to food-contact cleanliness, then extruded into clean rPET pellet.

04
🧪

Food grade

A solid-state reactor lifts the pellet to food-grade quality, the highest-value, most defensible product in the market.

05
♻️

The loop closes

Pellet and fibre go to global brands. What can't be recycled becomes bricks, roofing and community products. Nothing is wasted.

Why it matters

Every loop closed
changes a life.

The community isn't a side-project bolted onto a plant, it is the business. Collectors are paid fairly and instantly through the app. Each site creates around forty skilled, safe jobs, from managers and operators to drivers and security. Cities get cleaner, worker housing gets built from recycled bricks, and a formal economy grows where there was none. As MlimaLoop scales across the country, so do the people and the towns around it.

Why MlimaLoop

The competitive advantage.

First integrated recycler of its kind in East Africa.

First-mover infrastructure

No integrated rPET operation of this kind exists in East Africa. We are building the infrastructure the region's circular economy depends on, first to build, first to scale.

02

Food-grade is the prize

Food-grade pellet commands premium, long-term offtake from beverage and FMCG brands, the most defensible position in the market.

Ocean-bound credits

Owned collector networks and buying stations mean cost-controlled, reliable supply, not spot purchasing, and a feedstock stockpile that grows daily.

Roadmap

Built on evidence.

MlimaLoop is not an idea on paper. The foundations are already in place, and the path from here is a proven model repeated.

Done · foundation

Company, app, fleet, licences, feedstock

Registered Tanzanian company, the collection app built, five vehicles on the road, operating licences obtained, and feedstock stockpiling on site and appreciating in value.

Next · build Arusha

Equipment installed, two shifts from day one

Full processing line to the SSP reactor plus a pilot yarn line and on-site QC lab. Non-food pellet sells immediately; feedstock built up means full volume from the start.

Then · certify

GRS and food-grade certification

Certified output and long-term offtake with international brand partners, unlocking premium pricing and premium plastic credits.

Scale · national rollout

Replicate to twelve sites across Tanzania

Moshi, Dodoma, Dar es Salaam and beyond, each new site funded from the cash the business already generates.

Platform

License the model and the app

Once proven, the system, brand and technology become a platform other cities and operators can adopt.
